Privacy Policy

Project Manager helps groups coordinate tasks, availability, and meetings. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Before publishing: Replace YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL below with an email address you monitor, and review this policy against your deployed app and actual data-handling practices.

1. Information we collect

When you sign up or use Project Manager, the app may process:

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, and authentication details provided by Supabase Auth or Google Sign-In.
  • Profile information: information you choose to enter, such as display name, role, student or ID number, address, contact number, Discord handle, and birthday.
  • Group and work data: group names and membership, tasks, due dates, priorities, assignments, Kanban columns, and availability slots you enter.
  • Meeting information: meeting title, date and time, description, invitee email addresses, invitation status, and a Google Calendar event identifier when an invitation is created.
  • Preferences: your chosen calendar color and light or dark appearance.

2. Google Sign-In and Google Calendar

You can sign in with Google through Supabase Auth. Google Sign-In is separate from connecting Google Calendar.

If you explicitly connect Google Calendar, Project Manager asks for permission to read calendars and calendar events and to create or modify calendar events. The app uses that access to list calendars you can access, display events from a selected calendar, and create meeting invitations with the people you choose.

In the current browser implementation, Google Calendar access tokens are held in page memory rather than saved to the Project Manager database. The selected shared calendar ID and event IDs associated with meetings may be stored in Supabase. Connecting Google Calendar does not automatically import all your personal events into the Project Manager database.

Google API data: Project Manager uses information received from Google APIs only to provide the user-facing calendar and meeting functionality described above. It does not sell Google user data or use that data for advertising. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and maintain sign-in sessions.
  • Display group tasks, schedules, directories, and meeting details to authorized group members.
  • Calculate shared availability from members' manually entered busy slots.
  • Remember profile settings and group selection.
  • Create calendar events and send invitations when you request them.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the app and protect its users.

4. Who can see your information

Other members of a group can see the group-related information the app makes available to them, including your display name, profile details shown in the Directory, task assignments, and availability. Meeting invitees may receive the meeting information you enter through Google Calendar. Do not enter personal details you are uncomfortable sharing with your group.

Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure. Google handles Google account authorization and Calendar API requests. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply to their services.

5. Storage and security

Project Manager stores account-linked app information in Supabase and uses group-based database access policies. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their accounts and avoid sharing invite codes publicly.

Some convenience preferences, such as your chosen group and sidebar state, may be stored locally in your browser.

6. Retention and deletion

App information may remain stored while your account or groups remain active. If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL. We will review requests and explain any information that cannot immediately be deleted, including information needed for group records or meeting invitations. Deleting information from Project Manager does not automatically delete a Google Calendar event or an invitation already delivered to other people.

7. Disconnecting Google Calendar

You can stop using the calendar connection within the app by leaving the calendar disconnected and ending the session. To revoke previously granted Google access, visit your Google Account third-party connections and remove the application's access. Revoking access does not automatically delete tasks, groups, or existing meeting records stored in Project Manager.

8. Children and sensitive information

Project Manager is intended for collaborative study and work, not for collecting sensitive financial, medical, or other highly confidential information. If you're using the app on behalf of an institution or with minors, make sure you have the appropriate permissions and follow applicable rules.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.

10. Contact
